About Fanyi is a London-educated new media artist specializing in computational art and interactive installations. A 2024 graduate of Goldsmiths, University of London's Computational Art program, they founded *Ginger Studio* in China to explore the intersection of human connection, digital interfaces, and playfully surreal kinetic systems. Their work interrogates how technology mediates relationships through sensors, generative algorithms, and absurdist mechanical interventions.
